Print collection:
1 ev19-keytar-case-top.step
1 ev19-keytar-case-bottom.step
1 ev19-keytar-case-keyboard-shim.step
1 ev19-keytar-case-shelf.step
1 ev19-keytar-case-power-switch.step
4 ev19-keytar-case-side-buttons.step

How to print: 
- Print everything flat
- supports should be enabled only for the bottom half of the case 
- Bambu labs 0.20mm Standard @BBL X1C
- Let er rip!

What you need:
2 M1.4x3 for keyboard to shim.
6 M2x4 for screen to shelf and shim to shelf.
4 M2x14 or M2x15 to close the case. 
Electrical tape 
Precision acrew driver

Steps: 
1. Using the 2 M1.4x3 screws attach the keyboard to the shim at the bottom with the two screw holes.
2. Using 2 M2x4 screws attach the shim to the shelf.
3. Position the screen and attach using 4 M2x4 screws
4. Plug in the screen connectors other end into the EV19 board.
5. Bend the cables enough out of the way that the battery can slip under.
6. Flip over the shelf and run the screen cable very sharply into the cable management channel. Once run, add electrical tape to hold the cable in the channel.
7. Plug in the battery to the EV19 board
8. Tape battery cable flat on top of the battery.
9. Attach the power switch 3D print to the power switch such that the little hole will be near the bottom of the EV19 board.
10. Put EV19 board, power switch, and battery into the bottom of the case. The battery cable should be coming out of the battery on the left hand side with the battery protection circuit face up.
11. Put all 4 buttons into the button imprints on the left and right side of the case.
12. Add some electrical tape to the outside of the buttons and power switch to hold them in place while you finangle. 
13. Carefully place the shelf into the bottom of the case, the pegs should align to the screw holes on the board. 
14. Carefully place the top onto the bottom+shelf make sure the buttons and the power switch are aligned to the holes.
15. Tightly hold the top and bottom case closed and use 4 M2x14 or M2x15 screws to attach the bottom and top together, the screw should slide into the hole until it hits the top screw holes where it will need to be driven into the plastic.
16. Congratulations! Enjoy your EV19.
